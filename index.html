Biography

I am a compiler engineer at GlobalFoundries, working on ASIP Designer’s extensively customized LLVM/Clang-based C/C++ compiler. My work spans the Clang front end, LLVM middle end, and custom backend tooling, with most of my work focused on analyses, transformations, and optimization-pipeline support. I am particularly interested in alias and memory-dependence analysis, code-restructuring transformations, and optimizations that take advantage of processor architecture.

Before joining GlobalFoundries, I worked on the same ASIP compiler technology at Synopsys until the business transferred in June 2026. The compiler dynamically supports processor targets generated from architecture descriptions, including RISC-V-based processors and highly specialized DSP architectures.

I completed my PhD at Delft University of Technology in 2023. My research focused on programmer-friendly intermittent computing and combined compiler transformations, processor architecture, and embedded systems. This work resulted in publications at PLDI, ASPLOS, and UbiComp.
